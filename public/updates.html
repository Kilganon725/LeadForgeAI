LeadForge AI updates and release history

LeadForge AI helps foreign trade teams turn a product, target market, and company profile into ready-to-send outreach. This page shows what each version added and why the product exists.

Purpose
Generate outreach copy for export sales development.
Workflow
Input a product, country, and buyer type, then generate content.
Output
Email, WhatsApp, follow-ups, and bilingual review copy.
